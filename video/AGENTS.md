This is a required *lookup and suggestion*, NOT a requirement to insert an image or change an approved creative pipeline.**

## Mandatory preflight (lightweight and fast)
1. Review the exact video script, spoken line, scene/shot plan, named entities, story bible (where applicable) and the video's visual restrictions.
2. **Search titles, categories and source fields in the shared master index FIRST** for candidate art relevant to each scene. Use the pack index to locate candidate JPEGs. Do not download all 18 ZIP packs to answer a one-shot query; avoid duplicate storage.
3. Suggest up to 3–5 plausible library matches only when *visually, historically, narratively, stylistically and legally appropriate*. Record the image ID, pack, source, license and exact shot purpose in the scene plan. Verify the actual image before selection. Deduplicate and respect reuse limits.
4. If no suitable candidates exist, say **“Library checked — no suitable image”** and use the project's pre-existing approved creation/sourcing workflow. **Never force stock or public-domain art into unrelated shots merely to save production time.**
5. Use museum/source rights information and the index as starting evidence, not blanket warranties; confirm commercial-use rights for the selected artwork and embedded marks/depicted content. Properly credit sources when required. Never use user-supplied or third-party channel videos as source material without rights.
6. Make the library suggestion visible on the preproduction shot list or scene plan: `Shared visual library: checked / shortlisted (IDs…) / no suitable match`. Do this before generating new images. Do not present a library match as a finished or approved asset until visually checked.

## Precedence and safeguards
- **Audiobooks:** owner-approved FULL-COLOR, original, story-exact 2D/manhua character/panel and visual-continuity rules still govern. Historical monochrome ink paintings must NOT replace required full-color story-specific illustrations. Optional artwork is subject to the latest audiobook visual standard and explicit story compatibility.
- **History documentaries:** image must actually depict/relevantly evidence the named subject or be honestly labeled illustrative. A historical Chinese painting is not evidence for unrelated history. Preserve footage ratios and source-first authenticity rules.
- **ASMR:** prioritize actual filmed / generated synchronized actions and matching sounds per its scene/audio gates. Static paintings are usually unsuitable.
- **Other animation, Shorts, promotional and editing projects:** consider only relevant images under each project's channel-specific footage, color, motion and rights restrictions.
- This recommendation **does not** change narrator, image generation quotas, scene timing, color standard, asset ownership, upload permissions, or any already approved videos. Do not publish new videos solely due to a library suggestion.

The preflight is a **source-selection convenience** across ALL video projects; it is not authorization to upload, publish, or override stricter project rules.
