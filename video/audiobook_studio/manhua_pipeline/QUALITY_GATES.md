# Audiobook Studio — current visual, audio and delivery quality gates

The [CURRENT_PRODUCTION_DEFAULT_2026-11-08.md](../CURRENT_PRODUCTION_DEFAULT_2026-11-08.md) and [COLOR_FIRST_PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md](../COLOR_FIRST_PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md) are authoritative. This is the ONLY active visual gate; all superseded narrator, multi-GPU and fixed-density requirements are deleted and must not be revived.

1. **Canonical inputs:** latest APPROVED manuscript, matching story bible, preceding episode continuity, exact spoken script, matching Kokoro Michael audio, cast and color references, rights record. Stop if essential approved assets cannot be verified.
2. **FULL-COLOR:** original story-accurate illustrated 2D imagery from the start. Review actual pixels for proper colors, identities, ages, costume, location, props, actions and continuity. Unrelated, duplicated, monochrome and generic images fail. Keep image assets in private Google Drive, never inline galleries.
3. **Scene timing:** one manifest row per cropped 16:9 panel shot containing story_id, episode_id, page_id, panel_id, image, image_sha256, source_excerpt, source_sha256 and measured seconds. Concatenated excerpts exactly reconstruct the whole spoken script. Picture length follows real narration and event timing, not a picture-count quota.
4. **Technical validation:** `color_panel_policy.py` validates pixel color and traceability. `scene_locked_quality.py` checks source locks; `assemble_existing.py` assembles CPU FFmpeg output to H.264 1920x1080/24fps with AAC. No synthesized filler, skipped words, hardcoded timing, duplicated adjacent panels or second-speedup. Inspect final audible/video output.
5. **Human review and delivery:** verify both artwork semantics and full episode audio, retain immutable input hashes and permissions, deliver a real verified link to PRIVATE Drive, and keep publication blocked until the owner's separate release instruction.
6. **Mandatory Mythveil audiobook melodic bed (9 Nov 2026):** every newly produced audiobook episode includes a quiet, mood-appropriate, rights-cleared instrumental melody underneath narration, with smooth fades/loops and ducking as needed. Listen to the final AAC/MP4 on headphones and a phone speaker: reject missing music (outside intentional dramatic silence), unintelligible words, overpowering melody, vocals, clipping or abrupt level changes. Record music provenance. This rule is audiobook-only, not ASMR/history, and does not automatically trigger edits to already published videos.
