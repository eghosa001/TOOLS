# Audiobook Studio — UNIVERSAL video production standard

**Approved 8 November 2026. Applies to EVERY current and future original audiobook story, title, genre, chapter, volume, and episode. No story is the permanent default.** This is the first production document to read in any new chat, after the repository `AGENTS.md`. It overrides conflicting older *Audiobook Studio* GPU/Qwen-first implementation directions, without changing separate ASMR, history or editing projects.

## Non-negotiable upstream prerequisite — complete illustrations FIRST

Read and obey [the owner-approved full-story illustration-first rule](FULL_STORY_ILLUSTRATIONS_FIRST.md). For **every story**, complete and inspect the entire story-wide illustration library across **all chapters/episodes**, store the source-linked artwork and **automatically owner-preapproved** manifest in private Google Drive, and verify the entire pack before starting **any narration or compilation**. The previous episode-by-episode art-plus-voice generation order is superseded; no partially illustrated MP4 is a finished video. Fail closed only on incomplete, mismatched, stale or corrupt art; owner approval for all generated illustrations is standing and automatic.

**Standing owner authorization:** Every image generated by the assistant is preapproved. Generate and save the images without showing a separate approval stage or asking the owner to review individual images. Automatically record `owner_artwork_approval: blanket_preapproved` in the complete-story manifest. This is not permission to publish to YouTube.

**Visual cadence and privacy:** Never show generated illustrations in chat. Save them directly to the private Google Drive library. The 24-beat storyboard is NOT a 24-image limit; build original, individually source-matched shots every approximately 5–8 seconds of spoken narration, with no still held for more than 10 seconds, and reject video compilation when this fails. See [the full-story illustration rule](FULL_STORY_ILLUSTRATIONS_FIRST.md).

## Deliverable and fastest reliable route

**Deliver a finished, watchable video, not image galleries, storyboards or repeated plans.**

1. Identify the story the user requested. Fetch its **latest approved full manuscript, exact episode text, character bible, pronunciation lexicon, shot notes and previous approved assets** from private Google Drive. Do not pick a novel simply because it was the latest example. If no story was named, use a uniquely identifiable in-progress approved episode; otherwise ask only the essential disambiguating question.
2. Break the **verbatim** episode into many short, consecutive source-linked VISUAL shots (approximately 15–20 spoken words each), not only broad chapter/storyboard beats. For every beat, note the *exact narration text*, required subject/action/location/time/world-state, what must NOT be shown, and persistent identity references. Image count follows narration duration and action changes; roughly 180–288 distinct images for 24 minutes at 5–8 second cadence. Never substitute 24 minute-long stills.
3. Make original **2D Chinese manhua-style still illustrations** using ChatGPT's built-in image generation. Use the particular story's reference portraits, wardrobe, settings and power-system bible. Generate only missing/rejected assets. **Never generate 3D scenes, AI animation, moving-character clips, unrelated 'fantasy wallpaper', text-heavy infographics or contact sheets as finished shots.** No need to find a GPU or open another image service.
4. Internally check **every actual illustration**, not just its prompt, against the text spoken *at that exact time*. Reject inaccurate cast, props, clothing, age, injuries, power level, chronology, location or inappropriate fantasy effects. Present-vs-flashback, present-vs-vanished objects and internal-vs-external injuries are examples of **universal consistency checks**, not one story's canon. Each story supplies its OWN positive/negative constraints.
5. **Only after the whole-novel illustration gate passes**, produce or reuse expressive **English male Kokoro Michael (`am_michael`) narration on CPU**. Use **approximately 1.25 synthesis speed** as a working starting point and audit the actual speech; do not double-apply tempo. Correct fictional proper names and special terms using full-sentence auditions and each story's lexicon. A different narrator is allowed only on explicit owner approval.
6. **Only after all story-wide illustrations are complete and source/hash-verified**, set image starts/ends from **measured narration beat durations**, not estimated word counts or arbitrary 15–30s slots. Use simple image cuts/short fades, optional restrained framing/zoom only if it adds clarity. Assemble on CPU with FFmpeg to **1920×1080 16:9 H.264/AAC MP4**. Do not add animation or GPU services.
7. **Quality gate:** exact approved source coverage, no missing/duplicated speech, actual images appropriate to the spoken sentences, consistent world/characters, natural voice, name pronunciation, real audio-video synchronization, no silence/clipping, rights review, full video review and technical validation. Correct only failed beats and reuse passed files. An automated hash test **cannot verify artwork semantics**.
8. Upload the **real finished MP4** to the story's **private Google Drive production/review folder**, verify its link, and give the user the VIDEO link first. Do not send separate artwork unless explicitly requested. Do not claim a preview is the full episode, or a test pass is audience approval. **Never auto-publish to YouTube.**

**Google Drive is storage and source-of-truth, not an image generator.** Built-in image generation supplies the 2D artwork; free CPU (local or existing GitHub Actions, when available) handles Kokoro speech and FFmpeg. Avoid payment trials, GPU account research, extra tools and new infrastructure unless an actual unsolved production dependency demands them.

## Story-specific information stays separate

**These are UNIVERSAL rules. There are NO permanent character names, locations, cultivation realm names, spiritual roots, novel titles, script URLs or story-specific visual demands in this document or in shared code.**

- Each story has a private **story profile**: approved script/version/episode boundaries, stable character portraits, precise visual continuity, setting and power system (if present), pronunciation dictionary, allowed/forbidden visual elements, review folder, and asset hashes.
- Retrieve the right profile from private Google Drive by the requested story ID/title. A nonbinding [story source index](STORY_CONTEXT_INDEX.md) records known pointers; it is **not** a queue, permanent story choice, or authorization to change canon.
- Source changes affect only the related story/episode. Cache keys and working directories distinguish story + episode + source + model/speed + asset hashes. Never use one story's artwork, labels, character data, rules or lore in another.
- **Cultivation metaphors apply only to novels that actually contain them.** Horror, science fiction, adventure or mystery chapters get images matching their own scenes and world rules, while retaining the same production pipeline.
- Do not put unpublished manuscript text, private character bibles, images, or spoilers into public GitHub commits or action artifacts.

## Quality checks and fail-closed behavior

| Gate | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Source | Approved episode + exact SHA-256; complete, ordered spoken beats reproduce every word |
| Voice | Correct story-name auditions, expressive pace, measured durations, cached/validated 24 kHz audio |
| Visuals | Exact spoken beat, story-specific must-show/must-NOT-show, real image inspection, consistent cast and world, image SHA-256 |
| Timeline | Measured per-beat speech duration; no gaps, overlaps, incorrect story periods or lingering unrelated art |
| Media | Opened and reviewed MP4 with audible speech, 1080p H.264/AAC streams, sane levels, pacing and cuts |
| Delivery | Verified private Drive MP4 link; distinct owner approval required before YouTube release |

**Fail closed** on actual uncertainty or rejection, but do not waste time regenerating already-approved art or rerunning completed audio. No arbitrary word/image quotas, no padding, no claims of a finished video until it exists and is checked.

Programmatic enforcement: [`scene_locked_quality.py`](manhua_pipeline/scene_locked_quality.py), [`kokoro_cpu_episode.py`](manhua_pipeline/kokoro_cpu_episode.py), [quickstart](manhua_pipeline/KOKORO_CPU_QUICKSTART.md), regression tests, and CPU FFmpeg assembler. The validator verifies source and attested visual evidence; it **does not itself interpret images**. Final editorial review of the actual pixels and audio remains necessary.

## New-chat instruction (universal)

Read `AGENTS.md`, `video/audiobook_studio/AGENTS.md`, this standard and the chosen story's private Drive profile; resume **that** story's next requested/approved episode with existing assets. Do not restart from scratch, invent prompts/images for display, suggest a GPU hunt, or default to the last example novel. Generate checked images, synthesize/reuse speech, assemble and verify the video, upload it privately, and deliver the MP4. If any step is genuinely blocked, report the precise blocker and what actual output exists, not a fabricated completion.

The owner's latest explicit approval and source canon always outrank any older implementation example. Retain the original writing-quality master rules and scorecard for story craft. **This production method is unlimited across stories; only each story's private profile changes.**
