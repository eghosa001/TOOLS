# Legacy whole-novel-first and dense-shot policy — SUPERSEDED

This file documents an EARLIER 8 November 2026 approach that required illustrating an entire novel before any current-episode narration/video and 180–288 individual images for a 24-minute audiobook, with a 5–8 second change interval and 10-second maximum still hold.

**DO NOT ENFORCE THOSE REQUIREMENTS ON NEW STORIES.** The owner subsequently selected a far more efficient and proven approach: original FULL-COLOR multi-panel pages, separately cropped and narratively timed shots, cached Kokoro narration and one automated FFmpeg assembly. No monochrome images, no enforced per-minute image count, no full novel art backlog before the requested episode. See COLOR_FIRST_PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md and CURRENT_PRODUCTION_DEFAULT_2026-11-08.md.

Preserve intact the still-valid principles of source-exactness, private story assets in Google Drive, hash-verifiable manifests, technical QA, continuity, rights, owner preapproval of image generation and separate YouTube publication permission. The 109 master rules for writing remain authoritative for story craft unless a later explicit owner decision overrides them.

This historical document remains under the old name so old linked discussions can be understood; repo history retains its original contents. It is NOT a production runbook.
