# Mythveil Audiobooks — Audio-First Production Standard
Owner directive: 14 November 2026. **Applies to future episodes and unfinished productions.** This supersedes older mandatory scene-illustration, full-colour manhua, per-shot image, and OpenMontage storyboard requirements whenever they conflict. Existing published videos are not automatically changed.

## Channel and episode format
- Keep the existing channel, **Mythveil Audiobooks**, the existing verified channel ID, each novel's canon, official titles, numbering, playlists, and existing public video IDs.
- Produce **full-length fantasy fiction audiobooks**, not motion comics or animated manhua. Keep the natural chapter/episode length dictated by the story; do not impose an arbitrary 8- or 12-minute maximum.
- Each video needs a clear, rights-verified **single book-cover design or quiet branded still background**. A simple waveform is optional. This visual remains consistent and must not depict invented plot events. Keep a real episode-specific cover/thumbnail; **no scene illustrations, character cutout packs, continuity renders, image-count targets, storyboard prompts, or visual beat synchronization**.
- Do not generate, reuse, order, or require the previous scene illustrations for any future production. Mark old scene-art folders and visual handoff documents RETIRED, not current sources. Preserve manuscripts, story bibles, pronunciation dictionaries, mixes, finished masters, QA ledgers, approved covers, licensed-source and copyright records.
- **Do not permanently delete legacy art until a file-by-file inventory confirms it is only redundant scene artwork, not an approved thumbnail, a master embedded in a Google Doc, licensed provenance, a required source, or an asset needed to reproduce an already-published video.** Never delete finished YouTube uploads to remove embedded illustrations; changing video frames requires a new upload and a separate owner decision.

## Story first
1. Read the latest explicitly approved manuscript and canon/continuity records. Maintain exact source/episode boundaries and existing character identities and plot facts.
2. Audit the first 60–120 seconds for immediate character objective, dramatic question, clarity and a reason to keep listening. Do **not** misrepresent the story by recording a teaser that never occurs.
3. Edit full chapters for meaningful decisions, sustained stakes, distinct dialogue voices, coherent action, payoff, audible clarity, and freedom from repetition or filler. Use the existing 16-dimension story quality rubric with evidence. Preserve approvals; store rewrites as distinct **working revisions** until accepted.
4. Do not shorten the chapters merely to optimize average view percentage, pad runtimes, insert generic cliffhangers, or add unearned twists.
5. Add episode-to-episode continuity and an accessible opening recap only where necessary. Read final text aloud before synthesis.

## Narration and audio
- Retain approved **Kokoro Michael (am_michael)** and existing owner-approved synthesis settings. Do not re-audition voices without a new request.
- Keep spoken text faithful to the approved script; improve pacing, pronunciation, chapter transitions, breath-sized sentence structure, and distinct dialogue delivery. Check names against the lexicon.
- Preserve the existing music-first rights/provenance step for the quiet underscore; keep it low enough for full intelligibility. No harsh transitions, clipping, long unintended silences, abrupt volume jumps or bad loops.
- Listen to **the entire audio** and the final encoded file using both headphones and phone speakers. A synthetic QA receipt is not a substitute for an actual human listen.

## Deliverables, release and monitoring
- Source-locked spoken script + story/continuity review, narration WAV/MP3, mastered audio, one consistent cover/thumbnail, single 1920×1080 H.264/AAC MP4, QA/evidence packet, verified private Google Drive link.
- For audio-first release packets use `production_mode: "audio_first"`; otherwise the legacy `illustrated` mode remains available for historical projects. Audio-first checks focus on story/audio fidelity, cover rights, frame legibility, full MP4 functionality, accurate metadata and a human listen; scene-art matching is inapplicable.
- **No public YouTube upload without explicit owner approval**, correct channel/series playlist, canonical title and verified live metadata. Never represent a private draft as a finished upload.
- Compare audience retention in the first 30 seconds, average view duration **and absolute watch time**, audience source, return listeners and sequel click-through across comparable long-form episodes. Do not attribute low retention to art without evidence.
