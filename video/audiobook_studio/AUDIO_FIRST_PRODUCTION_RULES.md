# Mythveil Audiobooks — Audio-Only Story Production Rules
Effective: 14 November 2026. This is the owner's latest explicit instruction. It supersedes earlier mandatory illustrated/manhua, per-shot visual, OpenMontage animation and scene-art requirements **for future Mythveil audiobooks**. It does not retroactively change already-published videos.

## 1. Format
- Mythveil is a full-length, audio-first original fantasy audiobook channel, not an illustrated story channel.
- Videos may display **one consistent book cover or branded background** for their entire duration. The cover can also be used for the thumbnail, with readable title and episode information. A subtle waveform is optional, not required.
- **Do not generate any scene illustrations, character portraits, scene manifests, visual continuity art, manhua panels, storyboards, visual prompt lists or image-by-image audio mapping.** The only artwork required is a suitable cover/thumbnail. No required illustration count.
- The listener must understand the entire story without looking at the screen.
- Preserve existing episode names, numbering, channel identity, release playlists and canonical titles unless the owner explicitly changes them. Existing public videos must not be deleted or reuploaded merely to change the image format.
- Do not delete existing art or raw video/source files. Their disposal is a separate action requiring a clearly scoped request.

## 2. Narrator: selection is OPEN
- Kokoro Michael is **not** an irrevocably approved standard for new production. Do not automatically synthesize another episode with it.
- Before selecting the new voice, test a **single identical, approved two-minute passage** in at least two suitable voices/engines, comparing naturalness, emotion, pronunciation, intelligibility, stable character voice, technical consistency, economics and repeatability.
- Modern cloud voices (e.g. Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS) and efficient offline engines (e.g. Kyutai Pocket TTS) are candidate trials only, not automatically approved. Record genuine voice/model settings, cost, permitted commercial use and a listening decision. Do not assume “free” means unlimited.
- Use one consistent selected lead narrator for each novel. Avoid unconsented real-person voice cloning. Keep narration clean, music subordinate, and verify pronunciation in full context.
- No narration production is approved merely by this rules update.

## 3. Rewrite fiction before producing new videos
- Retain the latest approved manuscript and story bible as the historical source of truth; preserve all prior drafts. **New rewrites are working versions until accepted**.
- Improve the actual stories, not merely surface wording: compelling opening, clear and consistent power systems/skills/levels, strong character desires and contradictions, meaningful choice and cost, sustained tension, human dialogue, coherent causal chains and earned endings.
- Follow the owner's 109-source-rule writing requirements and the 16-category Universal Story Quality Scorecard. The explicit visual-only requirements of the older rulebook are superseded by Section 1 above; the writing, originality, rights, continuity and QA rules remain active.
- Avoid stock AI narration prose, inflated metaphors, generic moral speeches, repetitive paragraphs, obvious exposition and filler. Keep scenes accessible on one listen and retain deliberate stylistic variety.
- Do not rewrite an entire series at once without identifying its latest approved canon. Start with the first story and evaluate full chapter structure and continuity, preserving accepted events unless the owner approves plot changes.
- Story quality must be evaluated against actual passages and cold-reader feedback. Do not invent independent reviewer scores or fabricate approval.

## 4. Outputs and release safeguards
- Production deliverables: source-locked manuscript and spoken script; tested narration/mix; a single cover; one working 16:9 encoded MP4; rights records; script/audio and final-MP4 QA; private Google Drive handoff.
- Release QA verifies complete spoken text, dialogue clarity, narrator quality, sound mix, real file playback, cover readability/licensing, original story rights, canonical titles, correct channel/playlist and explicit owner permission.
- **Never require scene-to-image alignment, animated storyboard output, illustration coverage or cast-appearance checks** for the audio-first mode. Mark old image-specific rules as superseded by this owner directive, rather than silently marking them passed.
- Current repository release validators/assemblers may still enforce the former image-only criteria. This rules update **does not represent a software change**. Any incompatible tool gate must be revised and tested separately before an actual audio-first release; never fake passes to push a video through it.
- Nothing is published publicly without explicit owner approval. Do not claim a produced file, private upload, narration test or story rewrite exists until it is actually completed and checked.
