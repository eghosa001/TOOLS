## Working brief

At 11:40 p.m. on 14 April 1912 Titanic struck an iceberg; she sank at 2:20 a.m. on 15 April. Eight researched chapters follow a 160-minute countdown: collision, safety assumptions, ice warnings, unseen damage, evacuation, distress calls, sinking, and investigation. The editorial angle is evidence-first rather than repeating myths.

**Hook:** "At 11:40 on a Sunday night in April 1912, a lookout spotted something in the darkness ahead of the Titanic. ... The vessel had just two hours and forty minutes left."
