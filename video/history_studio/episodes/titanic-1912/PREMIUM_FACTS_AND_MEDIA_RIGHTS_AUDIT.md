## EXCLUDED until paid licence / special permission
- **British Pathé historical moving images**, e.g. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=05o7sOAjtXE — licensing rights reserved: not a free stock library.
- **Woods Hole 1986 wreck film and digital stills**, e.g. https://www.whoi.edu/ocean-learning-hub/multimedia/alvin-visits-titanic/ — site says noncommercial use by licence request. Not approved for monetized YouTube.
- **2025 BBC/National Geographic Titanic scan or visualizations** — do not screenshot/clone merely because informational press release is available.
- **Francis Browne photos with unresolved international copyright term** — creator died 1960; a Commons file may be public domain in the USA but not every territory yet. Prior v2 visuals included one; premium edition filters it out where identified. Require formal clearance for exceptions.
- **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike or NC photos not independently assessed** — exclude from this commercial mixed-media composite.

## Photography/footage quality criteria (must pass before publishing)
- Do not claim a ship-other-than-Titanic as Titanic, particularly images from **RMS Olympic**.
- Preserve date/camera/photographer/source and differentiate **original historical photograph** from **original diagram** and **modern 2004 expedition footage**.
- Correct chapter mismatch: 2004 NOAA film is **not** original film of Titanic's 1912 sinking or the 1985 discovery.
- Photographic montage should not be used as independent proof of each constituent photograph unless each sub-image is identified.
- Every asset must carry clear rights metadata and be attributable by filename/shot ID before publication.
- Render should have intelligible speech, mastered consistent levels, no clipping, readable smartphone framing for Shorts, 1080p H.264/AAC and accurate chapter markers.
- Do not certify after automatic technical testing alone; inspect sampled frames and listen to random intervals across the full film.
- Thumbnail and title must reflect the content without sensational false 'original sinking footage' claims.
- Keep Zernio upload **paused until final candidate is reviewed**. Existing 68:27 v2 draft should not be published.
