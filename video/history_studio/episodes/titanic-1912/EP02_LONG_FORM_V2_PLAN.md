Target: **70–75 minutes** of substantive film, anchored by **8,800–10,200 sourced words**, measured against actual final narration WAVs (not fixed loops or intentionally slowed speech). 16:9 1920x1080, H.264/AAC. ## Fact bank (confirm on-screen and cite)
- 11:40 p.m. Apr 14 collision; 2:20 a.m. Apr 15 sinking (ship's clock as recorded in UK report): https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepMessages.php
- British report identifies ice warnings from Caronia at 9 a.m. and Baltic at 1:42 p.m.; messages require precise context: https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepMessages.php
- British report identifies six forward compartments damaged / flooding; exact breach geometry uncertain: https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepExtent.php
- Twenty boats with combined authorized capacity **1,178**, considerably below persons aboard: https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/BOTRepBoats.php
- U.S. Senate investigation heard **82 witnesses** and included over 1,100 pages of hearings; other descriptions differ by counting conventions: https://www.senate.gov/about/powers-procedures/investigations/titanic.htm
- UK report criticizes lifeboat shortage and other failures but **not every class disparity can be attributed to one simple allegation**: https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepFindings.php
- 1985 wreck discovery and location by joint American-French team: https://www.noaa.gov/office-of-general-counsel/gc-international-section/rms-titanic-history-and-significance
- Magellan 2022 wreck survey generated a 1:1 digital model used for Nat Geo 2025 film; its footage **is not available to republish solely because it is publicly viewable**: https://www.dgepress.com/natgeo/pressrelease/national-geographic-reveals-groundbreaking-underwater-scan-unveiling-titanic-in-unprecedented-detail-challenging-long-held-theories-and-presenting-fresh-insights-in-upcoming-documentary-special-titan/
- UCL work announced Jan 2026 studies detailed digital reconstruction: https://www.ucl.ac.uk/engineering/ucl-mechanical-engineering/digital-reconstruction-and-resurrection-rms-titanic-sinking

## Licensed/usable source strategy
A new media register is required: local path, full source URL, photographer/producer, date, licence text, confidence, restriction, attribution, checksum, approval. Start with:
- LOC 1912 lifeboats headed to Carpathia (no known restrictions on reproduction; rights assessment still required): https://www.loc.gov/item/2001704327/
- LOC Carpathia rescue photos (no known restrictions on publication; rights assessment still required): https://www.loc.gov/item/98509237/
- LOC survivors on Carpathia: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2014691298/
- Smithsonian 1912 Southampton departure original: https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object-groups/titanic-group
- NOAA Titanic 2003/2004 wreck expeditions: https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/maritime/titanic/past_expeditions.html
- NOAA media copyright policy: https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/faqs/
- Wikimedia Commons: individually check original item license and all file-specific restrictions; avoid assuming whole category is public domain.

**Do not extract and reupload BBC, Nat Geo, Hollywood film, or independent creator's animation.** We may summarize their discoveries with citations and original explanatory graphics. If photos are unavailable, create clearly marked original educational diagrams and avoid confusing AI impressions with real archival history. Exact re-enactments must be labeled 'ILLUSTRATED RECONSTRUCTION • NOT ARCHIVAL FOOTAGE'.

## Filmmaking / quality gate
- ~150–210 *meaningfully different* shots / edits (archival photographs, verified period postcards, primary-document zooms, true period illustrations, animated source-derived maps, original diagrams), not looped Ken Burns pans of the same nine photographs.
- Provide source overlays where evidentiary claims appear, source list in YouTube description, contextual difference between a 1912 record and modern interpretation.
- Human editor must hear the actual full narration; existing generic Piper audition voice is not automatically the desired series voice.
- Integrated original / licensed subdued underscore with genuine dramatic pacing, gentle transitions, accessible captions; no sensationalist invented testimony.
- Conform long-form audio to consistent intelligible program loudness and avoid clipping; check title safe areas on phone and television.
- Render-check actual MP4 via ffprobe (duration >= 3600 seconds, H264/AAC, 1920×1080), check subtitle alignment, chapter markers and archive attributions.
- Record final reviewer approval of historical facts, image rights, narration quality, sound and real duration **before** publishing.
- Shorts should each include a hook, distinct high-value historical discovery, subscribe CTA and specific linked/full-video reference after publication.
- Upload is **paused by user** until the user has received and reviewed the complete long-form version; Zernio may be used only after approval.
