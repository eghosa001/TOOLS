# Editorial premise
A 4,500-year-old monument appears to guard a secret. Some of its hidden spaces were discovered by cosmic-ray detectors, while a papyrus journal discovered far from Giza tells us how workers moved its stone. A lost Nile branch may explain its geography. The engineers' exact building system is still debated. The distinction between archaeological evidence and unsupported spectacular claims is the narrative spine.

Do not claim that a treasure, mummy, ancient technology or secret civilization has been found inside the 2017 void or 2023 north-face corridor. No extraterrestrial/supernatural claims as fact. No confusing Khufu's Great Pyramid with Khafre's pyramid, or scientific muon detection with disputed satellite-radar claims.

# Evidence ledger with editorial guardrails
1. **Great Pyramid baseline / established.** Egypt's Ministry describes Khufu's Great Pyramid as a Fourth Dynasty royal tomb, originally ~146.5m high, with three principal chambers. Building duration remains approximate. Source: https://egymonuments.gov.eg/monuments/the-great-pyramid/
2. **Merer / direct ancient documentary evidence.** Wadi al-Jarf papyri include daily logs of a supervisor transporting fine limestone from Tura to Giza during Khufu's reign. The papyri document logistics, not a full blueprint for the pyramid. Source: https://www.history.com/articles/egypts-oldest-papyri-detail-great-pyramid-construction ; https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/711805-oldest-papyrus
3. **Heit el-Ghurab / excavation supported.** Domestic, industrial and administrative areas, faunal remains and worker burials reveal large organized labour infrastructure. Some remains show healed injuries. Important: identified town is chiefly associated with Khafre and Menkaure building phases; older Khufu layers are possible rather than wholly proven. Source: https://egymonuments.gov.eg/monuments/workers-town-and-cemetery/ ; https://aeraweb.org/projects/
4. **Ahramat Branch / 2024 peer-reviewed field and remote-sensing finding.** Segment of a ~64km extinct Nile branch near 31 pyramid sites, hypothesized to have aided logistics. It is not proof of every detail of Giza's construction. Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-024-01379-7
5. **Big Void / established detection, UNKNOWN purpose.** 2017 Nature paper finds a large void above Grand Gallery at least ~30m long, corroborated by three muon-detection approaches. Never label it a treasure room. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature24647
6. **North Face Corridor / independently distinct from Big Void.** Nature Communications 2023 and Scientific Reports 2025 identify and corroborate a corridor near the north-face chevron, with non-destructive imaging. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-36351-0 and https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91115-8 ; see additional 2025 ERT study https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-29081-4
7. **Pyramid robots / actual missions.** University of Leeds robotic expedition explored the southern Queen's Chamber shaft and observed red painted mason marks. https://robotics.leeds.ac.uk/case-studies/djedi-robot-archaeological-expedition/
8. **Khufu boats / museum evidence.** Two sealed boat pits discovered in 1954. First boat reconstructed over >10 years, 42.32m. Different hypotheses for symbolic/funerary use. https://gem.eg/collection/khufus-boats-museum/
9. **Sphinx / strong but not absolute Khafre attribution.** AERA quarry and temple relative chronology overwhelmingly favour the Fourth Dynasty Khafre construction sequence; dissenting older-date speculation needs clear evidential context. https://aeraweb.org/projects/who-built-the-sphinx/ and https://aeraweb.org/why-sequence-is-important/
10. **2026 earthquake mechanics / research interpretation.** Scientific Reports found structural/geotechnical features that may help account for seismic resilience; do NOT claim demonstrated deliberate earthquake-proof engineering intent. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-49962-6
11. **2026 retracted radar paper / confirmed retraction.** On 10 August 2026 MDPI retracted a 2022 radar paper for methodological flaws and statistical errors. The authors disputed retraction. The claims around a vast underground city are not established archaeological discoveries. Retraction source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/18/16/2679 ; see independent assessment https://factcheck.afp.com/doc.afp.com.42LQ7G3
12. **Debated construction mechanics.** Present transport via sledges, ramps and water routes as evidence-based methods, while stating that detailed full-build configurations remain uncertain. Smithsonian: https://www.si.edu/spotlight/ancient-egypt/pyramid . A critique of extrapolating a Hatnub quarry ramp: https://archives.palarch.nl/index.php/jae/article/view/339
13. **The "slaves built everything" narrative / nuance.** Evidence for large organized settlements, rations and trained labor does not answer whether all forms of compelled labor were absent. Do not make sweeping absolute claims.
14. **Original imagery copyright.** Harvard's Digital Giza is excellent for research, but research access alone does not make every image, 3D model or clip freely reusable in a monetized video. Verify each record's licensing.
