## Critical accuracy and rights exclusions

- **Do not use** [Met object 'Statuette of Merer'](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Statuette_of_Merer_MET_10.176.60_01.jpg) to illustrate **inspector Merer of Khufu**. The matching name is coincidental; the object is not established to depict this Fourth Dynasty inspector. A free photo with the wrong subject is still the wrong shot.
- **Do not use** [Heit el-Ghurab Google Earth screenshot](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Heit_el-Ghurab.png) merely because someone uploaded it with a CC BY tag. The underlying source is **Google Earth**, and the uploader may not control imagery reuse rights. Obtain a genuinely licensed original excavation photograph or use a clearly labelled research schematic instead.
- Avoid extracting visuals from AERA, Egyptian government sites, National Geographic, press images, museum marketing, Nature figures or television broadcasts without specific reuse rights for **that figure/photograph**. Public factual information and commercial visual rights are different things.
- Use authentic stills of surviving papyrus or actual Merer records **only if** the actual file is identified and its commercial licence or permission is established. Do not put an unrelated ancient papyrus on screen and suggest it is Merer's document.
- Do not turn the manuscript source title, filename keyword or search-engine thumbnail into an automated editorial selection.

## Practical manual editing sequence

1. Preserve all original spoken narration and 414 scene timecodes.
2. Open the [candidate boards](https://github.com/eghosa001/TOOLS/tree/giza/authentic-image-candidate-research-2026-10-28/video/history_studio/films/giza_hidden_history_2026/authentic_photo_research) and check exact source pages.
3. Select images individually against the actual passage, favouring source-original photographs over generic montage.
4. Record attribution, licence deed, clip treatment and asset checksum.
5. Apply `source_first_policy.py` plan and release checks, then render with real-image-biased pacing and review the full film before approving publication.

**No final picture edit should be generated simply by indexing these files or by lexically matching their names to the script.**
