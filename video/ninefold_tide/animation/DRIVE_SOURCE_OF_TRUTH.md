## Conflict and approval protocol

1. Open Drive project index and art index; review latest approved revisions and applicable story-continuity decisions. The authoritative item for a specific shot may be newer than a general series summary.
2. Record each source URL and the named person who actually verified it in the private shot manifest. Missing review must remain null.
3. Confirm a separately approved character turnaround; current Naya v10 and v12 are **not** approved final model sheets.
4. Copy only the necessary *approved* asset revisions into your private shot workspace, record hashes, and avoid re-downloading unchanged files.
5. Never put private raw character art, full scripts, or Drive access tokens into this public repository.

This file is a reference list, not a claim that any creative approval has taken place.
