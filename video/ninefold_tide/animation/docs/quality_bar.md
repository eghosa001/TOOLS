# The Ninefold Tide — shot acceptance bar

The benchmark is compelling, expressive, original hand-drawn TV animation with disciplined anatomy, staging and physically coherent action. Higher resolution and more generated frames alone cannot meet it.

The stage-specific visual review checks require evidence and a named reviewer for:
1. On-model identity: age, face, left-brow scar, braid knot and clothing never drift.
2. Age/proportions: Naya reads as a wiry 16-year-old, not a mature fashion illustration.
3. Anatomy/silhouette: credible posing, perspective and screen-readable gestures.
4. Hand/rail contact: fingers wrap over the actual rail correctly; believable grip and contact shadows.
5. Costume/props: storm-blue #355F73 jacket, teal #27585C tunic, cream #D7C6A6 measured tape; episode-correct notebook and Reading Disc.
6. Perspective/foot plant: camera, railing, body root and feet match physically plausible geometry.
7. Continuity/acting: eyes register strange tone before shoulders then hand; restrained alarm, not magic or action-hero posing.
8. Lines/paint/shadows: cleanup and color layers are intentional, backgrounds are professionally painted, not raw viewport geometry.
9. Background integration: physically correct depth, atmosphere, occlusion, sunlight, reflections and mechanical vibration.

Inspect frame 1, critical keys, neighboring in-betweens, the full ordered image sequence, 100%-scale face/hand crops, and phone-scale playback. Flag frame pops, missing drawings, accidental morphing, texture crawling, costume jumps and lighting changes. No automatic score can grant the human art gate.

Unverified v03/v04 puppet videos, v10 construction drawings and the v12 study **fail admission** until fully redrawn/reviewed. No one has certified a production-quality Naya drawing or final animated shot yet.

Release also needs rights, approved voice, natural lip acting, music/foley accuracy and complete episode viewing; shot export alone is not release.
