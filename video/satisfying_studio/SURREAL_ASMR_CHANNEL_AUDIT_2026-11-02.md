## Content and packaging observations

- **Niche clarity:** tactile water, rain, fire, sand and crystal/CGI textures.
  A consistent channel-wide promise can be "close-up satisfying visuals +
  material-matched sound." Do not claim all uploads are recorded nature
  footage, all are CGI, or any is `no talking` unless the exact master confirms.
- **Repeated generic wording:** several launch titles contain "ASMR",
  "Relaxing", "Satisfying" and "#Shorts." These words alone do not distinguish
  the sound and gesture. For **future** uploads, lead with the material and
  visible action, not identical generic SEO prefixes.
- **Water cluster:** three videos all feature crystal/rain/water in some
  form. On future uploads, differentiate "night rain on glass" from "flowing
  stream/waterfall" and "crystal flower" in the very first frame and first
  description sentence. Do not casually retitle a video receiving traffic.
- **Short vs long form:** the ten-minute flowing-water video is a different
  experience from the brief tactile clips. Compare age-matched Short metrics
  to other Shorts and long-form to long-form. The public Data API does not
  expose stayed-to-watch, impressions CTR or retention.
- **Descriptions:** existing Zernio publication records include unusual
  source/provenance wording for water/rain. This can be useful for licensing,
  but the viewer-facing two lines should communicate the actual sensory
  event first. Preserve required creator/source attributions and avoid
  removing them for SEO.
- **Artistic quality is the main product:** sound must follow real contacts,
  not randomly spaced isolated impacts. For marble extensions, continuous
  rolling and gentle repeated contacts should be tested against the supplied
  benchmarks; metadata cannot rescue unsatisfying sound.
