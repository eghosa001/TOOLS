## Production decision
**STOP at preview. Do not render or publish a 26-second master yet.**

A genuinely rendered, original 8-second **720×1280 / 30 fps** proof (time window 10.4–18.4s) has been produced and technically probed with H.264 video and 48-kHz AAC audio. It is only a **stylized 2.5D look-development test**, not a photorealistic premium marble run. The preview illustrates six deliberately choreographed glossy colour marbles rolling through a serpentine course, passing four fixed kinetic gates and entering six matching finish cups. Images and sound were synthesized from original procedural code; no third-party stock media were selected.

## Preview assessment and stop reasons
- Design is clean, readable, original and movement is apparent in inspected contact-sheet frames.
- It does **not** yet meet the desired premium photorealistic materials, lighting and physics standard.
- Audio stream presence, rate and encode are verified. Impact times were authored to correspond to gate crossings; **full listening approval has not happened**.
- Full rendering stays forbidden until a new representative preview genuinely earns >=9/10 visual, motion, sync and satisfaction scores after audiovisual inspection.
