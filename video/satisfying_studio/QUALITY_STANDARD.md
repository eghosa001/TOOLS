## Three separate original ASMR format families

**A. Surreal object reveal:** macro PBR metallic/paint tube, realistic squeeze deformation and visible contact, cohesive volume/geometry reveals an unexpected original material/sculpture. Surprise + consistent texture + soft click/pop.

**B. Tactile crunch/squish/spread:** vivid but photoreal lacquer, wet translucent jelly, soft porous foam or candy/glass-like shell; physically believable pressure, crack, tear, sticky spreading or release. Each material has a **distinct** Foley texture. Close lens and controlled hands if needed; no human-character/body animation required.

**C. Immersive fantasy surfaces:** richly art-directed surreal landscapes, pools, textures or environments, POV-style contact with believable local reaction. Lighting/volume/depth and sound extend naturally through space; don't make random interchangeable scenes.

A new shot can use one format or deliberately combine them. Do not recycle any creator's original video, original audio, faces, distinctive composition, protected characters or branding. Do not present inspiration as a remake. Every shot needs an original editorial concept and an authored treatment.

## Mandatory visual standard: artistic >= 9/10 in EVERY dimension

* **Photographic material realism:** use genuine three-dimensional surfaces/volume, well-tuned roughness, specular response, true refraction where appropriate, contact shadows and internal scattering where needed. No cheap cartoon/flat overlay; no "vector shape pretend-photoreal".
* **Macro detail:** credible microtexture and imperfections at the actual camera distance—peeling lacquer, fine pores, wet surface tension, tiny water droplets, fractures and fibres where the concept requires. No low-detail primitive disguised by post-processing.
* **Colour and light:** intentionally **rich, vibrant and beautiful** while preserving photographic exposure, readable dark/light balance, controlled reflections and scene coherence. HDRI + motivated lights + depth of field; no blown neon, flat diffuse shading, uncorrected grain or simple coloured silhouettes.
* **Physical continuity:** believable deformation/contact, material-specific crack/tear/squeeze, continuous object volume/identity and no floating/teleporting, snapping or pop-in geometry without a deliberately staged reveal. Slow, small motion is welcome; human animation is optional.
* **Cinematic composition:** immediate tactile opening, frame-filling action, one clear readable transformation per micro-shot, smooth and controlled camera, high-value ending/loop potential; no static poster pan disguised as video.
* **Technical minimums:** first look-dev can be lower resolution ONLY as an internal gate. Final vertical export: native **>=720×1280** (9:16 vertical HD). **720p is the default ASMR Shorts master**; 4K is neither required nor the measure of acceptable quality. Maintain intended sharpness, controlled motion blur, no obvious temporal flicker, jitter, banding, render noise, malformed fingers or mismatched light/refraction. Production source and rights/provenance must be preserved.

If any of these aspects is under 9/10 in the actual reviewed preview, **STOP**. Do not merely increase resolution or replace missing quality with generated artwork, generic filler, fake reviews or longer duration.

## Mandatory ASMR audio: equally important; >= 9/10

* **No unwanted near-silence of 0.45 seconds or longer in the actual final MP4** (including stock edits and mobile compilations); check each export using `production/audio_continuity_gate.py`. Small natural rests remain allowed; never patch with unrelated noise or fake contact sounds. An acoustically quiet scene needs a real, subtle source-appropriate texture or a different visual. This latest owner preference supersedes older language below allowing unspecified silence.
* **Real original or explicitly commercial-licensed Foley** selected by someone who *auditions* the candidates. An archive count is NOT an approved sound library. 426 recorded candidates currently exist, not 426 quality-approved cues. Add new recordings when missing.
* At each event, choose a physically convincing sound stack: primary action (squeeze, rip, crack, drizzle), texture microdetail (wet bubbles, tiny splinters, friction, shell grains), and subtle environment/room tone as needed. Silence is allowed where useful.
* Time each onset against the **actual rendered frames** with in/out frame and material source. No randomly keyword-assigned sound clips or guessed impact moments. A gentle squeeze must not sound like glass shattering; crunchy shell must not sound like a generic water splash.
* Edit with a clean **48kHz stereo** working master; remove unwanted noise, pops, clipping and harsh spectral peaks; use restrained EQ/transients, spatial perspective, carefully judged loudness and a clean natural tail/loop. Include no forced narration/music; optionally use licensed music only if the concept actually benefits.
* Monitor **through headphones** and visually inspect the rendered motion and transient timing on the editor timeline. Record exact Foley source path, creator, URL, license, SHA256 and cue frame. Audition layers in context, not individually only.
* A mix is **not accepted** if Foley sounds synthetic/unrelated, is out of sync, distracts from the visual, masks delicate details, or has clipping. A valid stereo/audio codec does not prove satisfying sound.

## Required quality-gated workflow — not a content-generation shortcut

1. **Manually design one original five-second shot** with scene, start/end, physical material response, 3D technique, studio lighting, camera, frames and exact planned tactile sounds. No large human animation is needed. No reference media copied.
2. Build a **real Blender** look-dev scene: authored mesh and contact geometry, PBR textures, Cycles shaders/refraction, colour grade, deliberate lighting, lens and actual camera. A high-detail *still* is checked first. If it looks rudimentary, do not animate it.
3. Run a tiny **motion/texture continuity check** with the chosen physically plausible deformation, keyed mesh / Geometry Nodes or simulated material as appropriate. Verify each critical contact frame; no jump cuts or unrealistic geometric growth. Production method remains **non-generative**.
4. Select, audition and arrange real Foley **per shot and material**; create a 48kHz layered track tied to the real frame grid.
5. Render **one 3–5s audiovisual preview** (the requested production is 5s). First preview may be 540×960+ and intentionally low-sample for technical assessment, but it **must never be advertised as near-reference quality unless the real reviewed content warrants it**. The 180s fail-stop budget applies to preview execution.
6. **Actually watch and listen to the entire preview**, comparing it with at least one suitably researched and personally reviewed popular video or an applicable creator-supplied example under the same criteria. Review must include: visual, material realism, microtexture, lighting/colour, physical coherence, motion, Foley quality, sound-to-frame sync and satisfaction. **Every score >=9/10**, blocking issues empty, real specific comparison notes and the **actual preview SHA256**. A script can check missing evidence; no algorithm can confer aesthetic approval or invent review.
7. Only then render the **true native >=720×1280 final** (default 720×1280; no unnecessary 4K upscale), under the separate explicit budget, and view/listen to every frame/beat. Re-score ALL categories >=9/10; record benchmark comparison, SHA256, source rights, audio cue sheet and blockers. User publication approval is a separate action.
8. **Fail closed**: If proof fails, return the actual defect report, not a cheap substitute; do not show a rudimentary test as "high-quality preview" or claim reference parity when unsupported. No scheduled job may self-approve or publish. Do not promise silent background production.
