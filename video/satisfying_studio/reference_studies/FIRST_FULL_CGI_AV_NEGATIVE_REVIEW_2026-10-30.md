## Verified corrective work

1. Audio-only 3-second timing proof reuses the original licensed audio and physically rendered frames, stretches the active material action to the full duration and was measured locally at **−22.7 LUFS / −7.6 dBTP**, with **zero interior near-silence gaps >=0.45s**. That is technical progress but is **not** a substitute for appropriately recorded normal-speed hit timbre.
2. Second native CGI render mode (`ASMR_CINEMATIC=1`) makes the camera track the active contact region, bringing the material and collisions closer in portrait view. It removes the unrealistic pips and uses warm macro fill lighting. It retimes real *evaluated Blender physics subframes* rather than repeatedly freezing the same image.
3. The sound engine now uses actual CC0 wood-on-wood rubbing/creaking recordings where visible physical sliding creates sustained friction, not arbitrary noise filler. It keeps per-sound provenance. None of this self-approves what was actually heard.
4. The CI workflow always uploads encoded MP4, proof frames, visual contact frame data, recorded sound SHA256/licensing and full AV scanner output. Reopen the actual result before making any quality claim.

## Accept / reject criteria for iteration

A frame is not premium merely because it renders or a sound is licensed. Before an approved full video: confirm visible contact within one frame of sound; source material and timbre authenticity; gentle-vs-heavy intensity parity, organic decay; no long unrelated silence; readable PBR grain and bevel silhouette; meaningful subject occupancy, consistent object structure and true motion; no frame interpolation ghosting; H.264 24fps, 48k stereo, peak <=−1 dBTP, full decoded AV diagnostic, and genuine nine-dimension >=9/10 full-video headphone review. **Fail closed**, record the issues and do not publish until corrected.

**No user-provided footage, voice or Foley is needed.**
