## Production takeaways grounded in the connected reports

1. **Sound palette must follow actual material and action.** Pressed jelly, foam tear, hard shell crack, sand drag, metal scrape and rubber squish should not share one re-pitched recording by default. A sound's start, texture and decay need to be checked against the rendered contact.
2. **Pacing belongs to the format.** The 21-second repair example has 11 reported scenes, the 9-second jelly example has two, the 70-second reveal has nine, the 5-minute ocean example has 76, and longform examples range from 42 to 123. The counts themselves show no single edit speed applies to every popular format.
3. **Material variety is allowed when the viewer can recognize an underlying sequence.** A comparison, a repair story or surreal-wide→immersed sequence can change materials. Random licensed stock shots without an intentional interaction structure and new matching audio are not the same thing.
4. **Do not treat all viral satisfying videos as quiet ASMR audio benchmarks.** The stress-ball example uses narrated storytelling; the ocean format is described as music-backed. For a pure no-talking/close Foley video, use the jelly, mango, kinetic-sand or longform texture examples for **format studies**, and independently audition the original sounds before claiming fidelity.
5. **Long-video automated scene notes need closer verification.** Provider scene endpoints and timing gaps are not frame-exact. A 123-scene report for a video previously listed as 20 minutes ending around 14:39 is an obvious reason to preserve uncertainty rather than silently call it full coverage.

## How future producers should use the stored sources

- Begin with [reference_library.json](reference_library.json) and [source_analysis_index.json](source_analysis_index.json), then open the relevant full [scenes/VIDEO_ID.json](scenes/) before planning any scene or Foley.
- Pick a **specific reference family**, identify the supported motion/audio patterns and record real timestamp anchors. Use them as **inspiration** for independently designed Blender footage, never recreate signature creator shots or copy source material.
- When the actual YouTube video or legally accessible media copy can be played, complete [REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md](REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md), use [frame_audio_audit.py](frame_audio_audit.py) for every decoded frame/audio window, and correct source timing or listening inaccuracies with exact file hashes.
- Preserve both provider-reported and independently observed versions, and explicitly mark disagreements.
- **No external original-video media or audio is committed**. The clips remain linked from their source.
