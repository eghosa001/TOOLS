## Proposed *original* five-second pilot

See [example_five_second_manifest.json](example_five_second_manifest.json) for the deliberately authored shot/story and sound beats. A cobalt/fuchsia metallic nozzle slowly extrudes translucent prismatic gel onto dark PBR stone. The gel spreads with believable contact, becomes a finely detailed crystalline bloom and settles; layered licensed wet/elastic/glassy Foley is matched to exact contact frames.

This is a **creative proposal**, not a claim that the current Blender look-dev already meets the benchmark. The previous primitive 2D marble-run and grainy glass-petal still were rejected as quality proofs. Nothing should be labelled premium or presented as reference-comparable until a real five-second audiovisual cut has been watched/listened to and passed the nine-dimension >=9/10 review gate.

Always use the standard's reference sound/compression and colour/texture lessons even for different creative concepts. No high-speed batch assembly, no automatically fabricated QC, no publishing without authorisation. History Studio remains separate and unchanged.
