# Evidence-led YouTube ASMR production research — 29 October 2026

**Scope:** `video/satisfying_studio/**` only. Read with `QUALITY_STANDARD.md`.
**Question:** What should genuinely popular YouTube ASMR/satisfying formats teach this project, beyond the four references supplied by the creator?
**Conclusion:** Consistent **sensory promise**, accurately matched **material-action sound**, visible progression and deliberate editing are reusable craft principles. *One physical material in every video*, *one universal cut speed*, *exactly three minutes*, and *music always/never* are **not** evidence-based universal rules.

## Evidence and limits

Sources are public YouTube Culture & Trends editorial analysis, official YouTube Analytics guidance, public video descriptions, and independently reported **dated** video counts. They are **not** frame-by-frame personal watching or headphone listening. A public title, thumbnail or total view count cannot prove an edit's exact pacing, Foley accuracy, algorithmic causation, audience retention, or underlying production method. Never invent those observations. For precise imitation-risk-free artistic benchmarks, commission actual licensed/in-app scene and audio review, record timestamps/strengths/weaknesses separately, and cite that review.

## Five production profiles — choose based on the video concept

1. **Single-material ritual** (`single-material`): slime folding, gel cutting, kinetic sand scooping, wax cracking. Same tactile family with controlled changes to colour, tool, scale, contact and intensity. Strong choice for relaxing audio-forward videos.
2. **Process/transformation** (`process-transformation`): dirty-to-clean, sculpting, repair, pouring/mixing to finished object, repeated craftsmanship. Multiple materials are fine if the process provides a narrative and audible logic.
3. **Controlled comparison** (`comparison`): 1-to-100 layers, different surfaces under *the same* press or slice, material A vs B. Differences are the point: consistent camera/tool/action make distinct sound signatures understandable.
4. **Curated themed compilation** (`themed-compilation`): several triggers tied by one clearly stated concept, sound intensity, palette or satisfying action; meaningful grouping, compatible levels and deliberate transitions. Not an unrelated stock-footage dump.
5. **Immersive surreal world** (`surreal-world`): fantasy surfaces and surprising physical changes that follow believable **internal** material rules and coherent perspective/acoustic space. Can vary substances while maintaining the designed environment. Produce with original Blender/free physics, not paid AI credits.

**Not universal laws:** no arbitrary cap on how many materials appear in a comparison or cohesive anthology; no ungrounded fixed clip length; no forced music; no assertion that repeating a *real action* with matching taps/crackles is intrinsically poor. A six-second identical clip repeated for 180 seconds without corresponding events **is** poor editing/audio. Legitimate repeated interactions can have legitimately similar sounds.

## Quality criteria that hold across profiles

- **Hook by sensation:** the actual action or credible anticipation appears promptly; no unrelated 8-second title preamble. Measure retention rather than claiming a universal number of seconds.
- **Tactile continuity:** viewers see *what makes the sound*; each prominent crack, squeeze, scrape or wet release belongs to the depicted material and contact. Synchronize event onsets to the actual final frames, review ear+eye; a one-frame target is a practical craft choice, not a published YouTube law.
- **Audible quality:** close recorded/licensed Foley, dynamic microtexture, intentional silence, controlled noise and true peak. Tapping or crack repetition is fine when naturally matched and artistically intentional. No unrelated five-second loop under unrelated video. Work in 48kHz stereo; verify technical clipping, but never mistake an FFmpeg pass for a human sound-quality endorsement.
- **Pacing:** allow slow compression and release when soothing; use faster clean cuts when satisfying; escalating layers/contrast for comparison; longer process reveals where warranted. Viewer expectation is the pacing anchor.
- **Visual clarity and payoff:** material reads instantly; camera/framing/light is stable enough to appreciate the trigger; change camera/movement deliberately. Conclude an interaction instead of cutting at the most important contact.
- **Source ethics:** original Blender/recordings or explicitly licensed assets, provenance retained. Never reuse creator audio/video or distinctive shots without rights. Apply YouTube's reused-content and low-value-content considerations separately.
- **Edit by evidence:** compare prospective concepts to **both strong and weak examples**; public views and channel sizes are imperfect signals. After publishing, measure content-family watch-through/retention and sound feedback, document what changed, update hypotheses. Keep clips and acoustic cues source-attributed.

## Practical commissioning protocol

Before production, a researcher supplies **3–6 direct public video URLs per targeted format**, current publication dates and view counts with collection date; at least one recent breakout and one established creator; and a short explanation of what is **verified** vs **hypothesized**. Editors review representative images and sound **with actual video access** before claiming benchmark-level visual/audio parity. Choose profile and write editorial thesis. Pre-plan timecoded interactions and source sound. Render a real 3–5-second audiovisual proof; fix sound **before** producing the whole episode. Release only after actual full master listening and watching. This protocol is comparative research, **not** copying another creator's footage or synthetic views, and is **not** a promise of virality.
