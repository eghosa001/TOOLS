# Continuous marble-chute ASMR: physical sound and long-form production standard

Purpose: fix sparse contact audio in the original seven-marble, five-second feeder shot without ever substituting unrelated or reference-video footage.

## Benchmark and measured starting point
- Compare each new review shot with current real-world marble runs from **Marble Run ONE** and **MARBLE BROS ASMR** (their sound is continuous motion through physical rails, bowls and obstacles). Do not reuse their audio or images.
- Existing 5.0-second, 24fps Blender chute: **7** verified first ceramic impacts, with frames 29, 40, 51, 62, 77, 84 and 108. The gaps include 24 frames (1.0 second) between the last two contacts. The actual AAC file starts with around 0.35 seconds of near-silence.
- More volume alone is not an adequate improvement. All important sounds must have a corresponding physical action.

## A publishable 30–60s version: generate new physical simulation, not loops
- Increase actual bead-release rate from ~1.4/s toward **3–4/s**, with controlled stagger, no overlapping initial marble positions and no bowl spill-outs.
- The suspended feeder should keep marbles visibly supported and feed the next group automatically. Add curving track/funnel surfaces where needed for intermediate rolling contacts and resonances, instead of having marbles teleport into the air.
- Model multiple genuine sections/camera moves, each with distinctive feed pattern and light changes; avoid tripling a 5-second render. Confirm material continuity across cuts.
- Simulate several groups/segments within one longer continuous physics bake or in distinct, individually rendered takes with an intentional reset between shots.
- For 30s, target roughly **90–120 separately simulated primary drops** before adding microcontacts. Adjust bead size and bowl capacity so the scene remains plausible. For 60s, use more than one collection area or periodically empty bowl **on-camera**.
- Keep physical glass, premium reflections, original ceramic bowl, 9:16 vertical composition and full 720p/1080p native frames; no interpolation or enlarged proof renders.

## Sound contact map (must come from real motion)
- Layer A: first bead–bowl impacts, timed from actual Blender Bullet positions.
- Layer B: shutter/rail movements and light metallic/gentle sliding sound, timed to actual gate keyframes.
- Layer C: marble–marble secondary collisions, detected from inter-centre distances and relative velocities; give these different, softer CC0 glass recordings.
- Layer D: very soft rolling/friction sound only when marbles are actually moving across the ceramic surface.
- Audio target for audition: **4–7 individually grounded audible cues per second**, with **no unexplained silence above ~0.25–0.35 sec while visible motion continues**; these are engineering targets, not claims derived from creator-view metrics. Brief pauses may occur when the scene actually stops.
- Use different lengths, subtle EQ, slight pan and level changes per contact. Do not repeat exactly one glass click 100 times at the same amplitude.
- Must preserve provenance for every licensed recording (CC0/explicit commercial terms). No music, speech, artificial reverb wash or unverified recorded material.
- 48 kHz stereo, exact motion–audio alignment checked per frame, mobile headphone audition, peak protection and reasonable loudness; render success never equals artistic approval.
- If physics produce only seven contacts, **do not invent 50 sharp impacts**. Use a fast-moving, multi-contact mechanical track and more genuine bead motion.

## Current local listening audition

It deliberately retains the original footage. It is **not** certified as per-contact accurate, and not approved to publish until visual/audio review. The future native Blender pipeline should instead emit event timelines from the actual 3D cache for all audio layers.

## Gate before YouTube
Required: original licensed source proofs, credible physics/no spawning, natural ASMR audio without long unexplained gaps, no clipping, vertical frame QA, and frame-level AV check. Stop rather than claiming premium quality when any gate fails.
