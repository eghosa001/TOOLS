## The film's story
**Logline:** One distinctive notched clear glass marble starts a connected kinetic sculpture. Its coloured companions follow gravity through a crystal spiral, precise mechanical gates, colour collectors, a fine bead cascade, a branching maze and delicate glass resonators. The original clear bead follows a separately visible bypass feeder and returns for one final quiet centre click that completes a rainbow mosaic.

**Sensory contract:** One physical world, one coherent sound environment. Close glass-on-glass rolls progress to individual mechanical clicks, a controlled high-density small-glass rainfall, isolated resonances, then a calm natural final decay. No narration, music, padded silence, CGI confetti, looping stems, real-world footage or paid AI-video credits. Recorded licensed Foley has to match the genuine glass/metal/stone/wood/ceramic surfaces and each final observed collision frame.

**Continuity anchors:** Same clear notched bead from first to last, consistent scale, physical fifth bypass visible from 02:50. Every module has an inlet/outlet or gate. Do not cut away before a physical payoff. Bead quantities must be traced from stage to stage, particularly the colour splitter and final ring.

**Photographic finish:** Charcoal studio, optically credible rich ruby/amber/emerald/cyan/cobalt/amethyst glass, satin brass, fine-bevel glass guide rails, Poly Haven CC0 HDRI plus physically motivated softbox highlights, controlled refraction, low clipping, readable silhouette and contact shadows. The 720p native floor does not establish 4K CGI parity; measure native 1080p feasibility on a representative proof before higher-cost rendering.

## FOUR independently directed portrait Shorts (each 00:30 at 24fps, native 720×1280)
**S01 — Crystal Spiral.** One sphere, two increasingly tight helical bends, one clearly observed collecting-bowl contact. Immediate opening glass entrance, physical path continuously visible, tiny recorded rail tick followed by genuine landing. NOT a crop/replay of Helix long-form source.

**S02 — Four-Colour Race.** Four distinct gravity routes and four physical pockets; each colour follows its own length/radius, the last amber sphere fills the final pocket at 25–30s. The sorting is geometric, not magical. Distinct portrait scene and Foley, not a montage of long-form shots.

**S03 — Glass Rain on Crystal.** Sparse first microbead contact becomes two denser streams across real ribbed glass, then thins to three deliberate final bead taps. Frame-readable particles and varied real glass-contact sounds; no fake shaker track.

**S04 — The Last Marble.** A rainbow wheel almost filled, a single open centre, the recognisable clear notched sphere moving down its own visible feeder and closing the circle at 23–28s. Different short-specific feeder animation from the feature film; final 2s quiet natural settling.
