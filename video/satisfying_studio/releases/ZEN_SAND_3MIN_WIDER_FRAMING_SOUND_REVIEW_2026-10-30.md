## Verified root cause: stock sources have **no usable synchronized original audio**

The eleven original Pexels source MP4s were recovered from the project's prior archived licensed footage. All seven with nominal AAC audio tracks measure mean/max approximately **-91 dBFS**, effectively encoded silence; the four other source MP4s have no audio stream at all. Thus neither the older remaster nor this new version should be advertised as on-location synchronized audio.

**Consequences:** Real recorded sand Foley can be a close physical match, but not independently guaranteed to line up with each original video contact frame. Previous repeated post-dubbed arbitrary cues made apparent mismatch worse despite passing no-silence technical gate. The next genuine premium long film should prioritize **new footage with synchronous physical audio** or original Blender rendered action with custom-made physical Foley and a human-reviewed frame-contact ledger.

Sand pour accents are retained only for sand actually flowing. Do not use water or heavy shovel sounds behind delicate dry-sand pours.
- Sound levels change gently with **broad movement measured from actual picture frames**. This is NOT a reliable detector of precise physical contacts. Source recordings are real; no synthesized hiss, text-to-audio model, arbitrarily named impact effect, or music pad is used.
- Low-level secondary *real sand* recordings cover gaps where the visual includes sand/grain movement; sound remains modest. A comprehensive headphones-while-watching review, including exact moment-by-moment match, has **NOT** been performed by an independent editor; user review is decisive.
