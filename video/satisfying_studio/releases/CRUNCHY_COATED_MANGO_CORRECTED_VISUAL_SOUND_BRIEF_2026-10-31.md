## Fixed new 8-second concept (provisional shot brief, not a promise of completed production)

- **0.00–0.70 sec:** 9:16 macro hero shot, ripe mango-shaped original object with rich rose-gold iridescent brittle glaze, clear uneven lacquer thickness, nuanced fruit shape, physically credible PBR studio reflections, soft shadow. No potato-like scan artefacts or big flat prop.
- **0.70–2.20 sec:** beautiful natural contact via an authentic 3D hand/press tool or correctly modeled slender knife; shell flexes locally with fine stress whitening before first crack. Delicate handling Foley only at contact; no crack sound until actual fracture begins.
- **2.20–3.50 sec:** first crack opens near pressure point, jagged branching **3D geometry**, tiny separate shell shards release with believable gravity and collision, flash of glossy orange gel underneath. Recorded sharp high-frequency *crack* precisely at first visible split (±one delivered 24-fps frame).
- **3.50–5.90 sec:** visible crushing/tearing continues, shell collapses in several credible smaller fractures, crunchy crackles with real physically timed energy rather than unrelated equal-volume repeated impacts. Revealed gel deforms, glistens and moves according to pressure; softer damp material sound must begin when gel is exposed, not before.
- **5.90–8.00 sec:** final clean piece release and sticky gel stretch/settle, a few quiet crackles for genuinely falling fragments, 0.4 sec of beautiful lingering finish and loopable last image. No unexplained continuous synthetic hiss.

## Explicit quality gate

1. Authored 3D scene and render provenance; source model and sound licensing verified, with downloaded SHA256s and attribution where required.
2. At least 9 spaced preview frames showing clear detailed physical progression from intact reflective lacquer through stress -> branched cracks -> visible detached fragments -> gelatin interior -> settled finish. Reject flat textures, potato-like surface, oversized box blade, floating disconnected shards, fake black UV islands, clipping and teleported halves.
3. For every discrete crack and wet compression event, annotate exact visual onset frame and peak sound frame; reject >1-frame offset and material-force mismatch. Need actual separate cracking and soft-core Foley, not the approved wet fig audio on shell cracking.
4. 24 fps, >=720 x 1280 (9:16); 48 kHz stereo audio, genuine original recording, no >=0.45s unintended silence. These are minimum technical specifications, not aesthetic proof.
5. Watch and listen to exact encoded master, headphone and phone, after render, compare to user's reference style and refuse public publishing if genuinely below >=9/10 artistic benchmark.
