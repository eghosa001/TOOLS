## Evidence safeguards

- PHerc. 1667 was *read continuously across its surviving inner portion*. The larger original scroll was already damaged. Avoid saying every page of the original scroll survived.
- The recovered work discusses ethics and appears related to Stoic philosophy; the author is not established.
- A different scroll, PHerc. 139, yielded the attribution *Philodemus, On Gods, Book Eight*.
- September 2026 lead-ink tests used modern experimental scrolls; do not present them as a complete reading of every ancient artifact.
